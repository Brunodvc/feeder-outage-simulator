This models a radial distribution feeder: one substation feeding a main trunk with three lateral branches, each serving two customers. Faulting a line segment cascades an outage to every customer downstream of it — closing the normally-open tie switch can restore isolated customers by re-energizing them from a different, still-powered branch, the same strategy utilities use to minimize outage scope during a real fault.
